# SpotifySorter — Design Context

The canonical design DNA for SpotifySorter, in short form. Every design skill (`impeccable`,
`animate`, `polish`, `critique`, `typeset`, `layout`, `delight`) reads this first. Depth lives in
`docs/design/record-shop/`:

- `brief.md`: the full DNA, with contrast ratios, per-page layout, the sleeve algorithm and motion.
- `foundation-system.md`, `foundation-graphics.md` and `foundation-data.md`: the APIs to build on.

The build spec, `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-23-record-shop-redesign.md`, wins over all of them.

**Superseded:** the earlier "private record-listening room at 1am" direction (coffee-brown dark
mode, brass, Editorial New and PP Neue Montreal) is retired. Don't design against it.

## Design Context

### Users

- One user, the owner. A personal music-curation tool, not multi-tenant.
- He sorts about 3,200 tracks from 100+ of his playlists into roughly 40 themed sub-genre and mood
  playlists.
- Specific taste (aesthetic rap, pop rap, sad slow songs) and strong design opinions. He rejected
  "AI slop" and wants the design skills used throughout.
- He prefers point-and-click to a CLI, but he runs the local Next.js dev server himself.
- He uses it in the evenings and at 1am with headphones on, as a deliberate task he wants to
  enjoy. It is also shown on interview screen-shares in lit rooms.

### Brand Personality

**Printed, crate-dug, generous.**

- Speak like a music nerd, not a SaaS marketer: "12 tracks", not "12 items".
- Be quiet by default and specific where SaaS would be generic.
- No exclamation marks, no "Awesome!".
- One word per concept everywhere. The threshold is the **fit** ("Tracks at fit 65", the Fit
  ruler).

Emotional goal: flipping through your own record shop, not managing a database.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Daylight Record Shop.**

- The categories are divider cards in one kraft crate, each with a two-ink risograph sleeve.
- The crate is drawn to scale, so it is also the library's chart.
- Apply is the moment the shop prints its playlists.

**Theme:** paper by day. When the OS is dark, an evening stock takes over: the navy becomes the
paper and a cream becomes the ink. Sleeves, divider tabs and album art keep their own stock. This
replaces "dark by default".

**Palette** (OKLCH tokens in `app/globals.css`; 0 failing contrast pairs in either stock):

- **Surfaces:** `paper`, `paper-deep` (wells), `card` (lifted stock) and `tint` (the open divider).
- **Ink:** navy `ink`, `ink-2` and `ink-3`. Every neutral is tinted to its hue, and pure black or
  white is never painted.
- **Action:** riso orange `accent` is the one primary action per view. It is never in a sleeve,
  and its label is navy in both stocks.
- **Status:** `success` (teal), `danger`, `warn`.
- **Crate only:** `kraft` and `hollow`.
- **Four temperatures:** each tints a divider tab and is explained by the printed key "dusk ·
  charge · glow · haze" (violet, pink, yellow, aqua). The temperature is guessed from the name, and
  the owner can override it with the Colour picker.
- **Nine riso inks** print the sleeves and nothing else.

**Type:**

- **Caprasimo** (a soft fat serif, roman only) for display: titles, the home h1, the sheet's
  category name, a dialog's price and the primary's label.
- **Libre Franklin** for everything else; list rows are 600.
- **Geist Mono** for catalogue numbers, stickers, the key and axes. Never for prose.
- Every stack ends in a CJK fallback. The scale is 1.25, with fluid display sizes and tabular
  figures (`.num`) in count columns.

**Layout:**

- The **site nav** shows on every page except `/setup` and `/label`: the wordmark with its record
  mark, Sources · Categories · Fit · Apply, and Labels behind a rule.
- Under it, every page with a main action gets the **top strip**: sticky, one row, the status on
  the left and the one orange primary on the right. There are no bottom bars.
- A 4pt scale with semantic names, a 1200px page and a fluid gutter. Breakpoints: `tab` from 721px,
  `desk` from 901px. Rhythm comes from contrast: tight inside a row, 64 to 96px between sections.
- **Home:** the headline sentence and one primary beside the crate, with "What Apply writes" (the
  press list) below.
- **Review:** list and detail. The bin sits beside one pulled side sheet on desktop; at 900px and
  below the divider opens inline.

**Motion** (framer-motion; tokens in `src/components/design/motion.ts`):

- Expo-out for entrances, ease-in for exits only. `SPRING_SETTLE` and `SPRING_SNAP` are
  critically damped.
- Transform and opacity only. No bounce, no loops.
- Three set pieces: scan (records drop into the crate), classify (counts tick on the tabs, never a
  song name) and the Apply credits (sleeves print in two passes), which are the one loud moment.
- The root has `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`, and every set piece has an opacity-only
  version. `PageTransition` stays a fade-in, with no `AnimatePresence` or exit.

**Components:**

- **Buttons:** the primary's ink keyline prints 4px out of register and snaps in on hover and
  focus. Quiet buttons are underlined text. `ink` is a card-and-navy button for `/label`, where no
  orange may print.
- **Inputs:** an underline with a visible label. A category name is Caprasimo on a dotted rule; a
  description is a ruled index card. Errors sit under the field and validate on blur.
- **Dialogs** are price tags: a punched eyelet, the price as the heading, no blur. Focus is
  trapped, Escape closes, and focus returns to the opener.
- **Rows** are liner notes: art or a sleeve with a 1px ink keyline, the title, the artist in ink-2
  and a mono catalogue number.
- **Missing art** prints the plain sleeve: flat, with no ink.
- **Focus** is a 2px ink outline. Every target is at least 44px.

### Design Principles

1. **Treat music with reverence.** Tracks are liner notes, and the track list is the product.
2. **Every mark is data.** A mark prints only where it differs from its neighbours, and the
   exceptional state gets the ink. With nothing to count, hide the column; never print zeros.
3. **One action ink.** Orange means "press this". Nothing decorative is orange.
4. **Print feel:** overprint, misregistration, paper tooth and keylines. Never gradients or glow.
5. **Quiet by default, loud once**, in the Apply credits.
6. **Blind labelling outranks the identity.**
   - `/label` is ink on card only: no sleeves, ink colours, art or motion.
   - No per-song score, rank or bin is ever shown anywhere new.
   - New per-song lists drop the labelling sample.
   - "Mostly" hides while the study is open.

### Anti-references

- A Spotify clone (green on black).
- Generic SaaS dark mode, purple-to-blue gradients, neon.
- Grey-card grids in the style of Linear, Notion or Vercel.
- "Spotify Wrapped" infographics.
- "Futuristic" anything.
- This project's retired 1am coffee-and-brass room.
- Mid-century wall-print motifs (a sun over a horizon, arches).
- Banned outright:
  - side-stripe borders wider than 1px;
  - gradient text;
  - glassmorphism and blur;
  - bounce;
  - the hero-metric template;
  - identical icon-heading-text grids;
  - the reflex fonts (Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, IBM Plex, Playfair, Fraunces and the like).

### Skills used during build

`impeccable` for each new screen, `animate` and `motion-recipes` once it's built, `polish` for the
final pass, and `critique` before calling it done.
